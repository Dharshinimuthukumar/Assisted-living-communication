🔬 Experimental Methodology & Research Question

100 Synthetic Assisted-Living Events

Research Problem: In assisted-living facilities, families face a communication dilemma: they either receive too little information or are overwhelmed by raw, clinical care-event logs that leak internal staff notes and cause unnecessary alarm.

Experimental Design: We evaluate 100 synthetic care events under two conditions:

  • Baseline Model (Current Status Quo): Raw care observations and internal staff notes are delivered directly to contacts without role authorization gates, consent checking, or medical safety classifiers.
  • Prototype System (Guarded Operational Pipeline): Events pass through role checking, consent validation, rule-based concise operational summarization, medical terminology interception, and human review gating.
