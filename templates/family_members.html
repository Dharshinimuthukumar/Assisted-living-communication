Family Role Policy Matrix

Primary Family Contact

Designated principal family representative. Receives daily routine updates, care activities, exception alerts, and holds full question-and-answer privileges.

Secondary Family Contact

Authorized family member receiving routine and activity updates. Exception notices depend on explicit resident consent permissions.

Emergency Contact

Reserved for urgent operational exceptions and safety escalations. Daily routine operational chatter is filtered out to avoid alarm fatigue.

View-Only Contact

Restricted family access. Can view general social and activity updates. Sensitive personal care logs and exceptions are blocked.
